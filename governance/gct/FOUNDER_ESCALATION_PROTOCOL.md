# Founder Escalation Protocol

## 1. Principle

Jamie Steeg, Founder & CEO, is the accountable corporate principal. Founder attention is therefore reserved for decisions that actually require Founder authority or strategic judgment.

Routine authorized execution should not be converted into a human approval queue.

## 2. Escalate when

Escalate when any of the following is true:

1. a reserved Founder power is required;
2. the authorized scope would materially expand;
3. an external legal, financial, contractual, reputational, or public obligation would be created;
4. a major irreversible resource allocation is proposed;
5. a safety-critical deployment or meaningful agent-permission escalation is proposed;
6. applicable GCL governance requires a Human Steward act by Jamie Steeg in that separate capacity;
7. the relevant authority, exact revision, evidence identity, or policy state is stale or ambiguous;
8. executive offices have a material unresolved disagreement whose resolution changes enterprise direction;
9. continuation would weaken or bypass a declared control;
10. available authorized diagnostic/recovery routes have been exhausted and the next step needs new authority.

## 3. Do not escalate merely because

Do not escalate solely because:

- a CI job failed;
- a connector temporarily failed;
- a compiler or test exposed a bounded implementation defect;
- an issue or PR needs ordinary triage;
- an artifact needs a clerical correction;
- a bounded experiment failed;
- a reviewer requested a non-material correction;
- an already-authorized operation needs retry/replay/readback;
- the next action is mechanically implied by an approved work package and existing policy.

The executive team should resolve these within delegated authority.

## 4. Escalation packet

Every escalation MUST contain:

```text
DECISION
One sentence describing the exact action Jamie must authorize or decide.

RECOMMENDATION
The executive cabinet's preferred disposition.

WHY FOUNDER AUTHORITY IS REQUIRED
Name the reserved power, strategic conflict, or ambiguity.

EVIDENCE
Exact artifacts, commits, analyses, costs, and material uncertainties.

OPTIONS
At least the credible alternatives, including doing nothing where meaningful.

REVERSIBILITY
What can and cannot be undone.

DOWNSIDE
Worst credible downside and controls.

DEFAULT IF DEFERRED
What the agentic team will do without new authority.

FOUNDER ACTION
Approve / reject / choose option / supply missing strategic constraint.
```

## 5. Default if no decision is available

When a reserved decision is pending, agents MUST preserve optionality and avoid manufacturing consent. They may continue separable, non-binding evidence gathering and preparation when doing so does not prejudge the reserved decision.

## 6. Distinct CEO and Human Steward capacities

Jamie Steeg may act as both GCT Founder & CEO and a recognized GCL Human Steward, but the capacities are distinct.

A corporate decision does not automatically discharge a Human Steward gate. A Human Steward authorization does not automatically allocate corporate money, sign a contract, or establish enterprise priority. Records should identify the capacity actually exercised.

## 7. Founder output

A valid Founder disposition should be transcribed against the exact decision packet and downstream artifacts. Agents may prepare the record but must not invent or infer Jamie Steeg's approval.
