# GCT ↔ GCL Authority Boundary

## 1. Corporate layer — Grand Challenge Technologies Ltd

GCT owns enterprise-level judgment and corporate action, including as applicable:

- mission and strategic direction;
- ownership and stewardship of corporate assets and subsidiary interests;
- portfolio selection and enterprise prioritization;
- capital and resource allocation;
- productization, commercialization, partnerships, licensing and venture options;
- contracts, corporate records, professional advice and external obligations;
- executive delegation and reserved Founder decisions.

Jamie Steeg, Founder & CEO (`jimsteeg`), retains ultimate corporate responsibility.

Agentic GCT executive offices may perform delegated internal analysis, planning, drafting, coordination and bounded execution. They do not acquire legal personality or independent power to bind GCT merely by being invoked.

## 2. Laboratory layer — Grand Challenge Labs

GCL is the operating laboratory subsidiary. It develops research, engineering, proofs, experiments, datasets, infrastructure, products and public technical artifacts under the applicable GCL governance and programme boundaries.

Where applicable:

- INTELLECT governs constitutional work-package/review structure;
- admitted GCL standards govern subordinate operating procedures within their adopted scope;
- programme-owned records govern programme status and evidence;
- MATHCERT owns mathematical certification within its declared routes;
- AETHER owns production semantic facts/cuts/provenance where its authority is applicable.

## 3. Mandate interface

The intended flow is:

```text
GCT enterprise question / portfolio decision
        ↓
bounded GCT→GCL mandate
        ↓
GCL research / engineering / review / evidence
        ↓
governed return packet with explicit claim boundary
        ↓
GCT enterprise disposition
```

A mandate specifies the decision-relevant question and evidence sought. It does not prescribe a successful scientific result or pre-admit a claim.

A return packet preserves the actual GCL evidence/claim state. GCT may decide to continue, accelerate, productize, partner, narrow, hold or retire an enterprise line, but that enterprise decision does not rewrite the underlying evidence.

## 4. Distinct human capacities

Jamie Steeg may exercise more than one formally recognized role across the GCT/GCL system. Those capacities remain distinct.

A GCT Founder/CEO decision does not automatically discharge a GCL Human Steward gate. A Human Steward disposition does not automatically allocate corporate capital, sign a contract, create a product commitment, or establish enterprise priority.

Records should identify the capacity actually exercised.

## 5. Forbidden inference

None of the following follows merely from GCT sponsorship, GitHub state, or executive preference:

- mathematical certification;
- scientific truth;
- production readiness;
- safety certification;
- data legality or training fitness;
- commercial viability;
- contractual authority;
- constitutional authority expansion.

Conversely, a technically successful GCL result does not automatically imply that GCT should commercialize or continue funding it.

## 6. Public/private boundary

This repository may publish the interface, public-safe governance descriptions, approved status projections and evidence-backed public claims.

Private GCT executive deliberation, portfolio rankings, capital planning, partner pipelines, professional-review queues, contract metadata, hiring analysis and Founder decision packets remain private by default.

Sensitive source records requiring dedicated secure custody should not be placed in either public GitHub or the private executive repository merely for convenience.
