# GCT Public Executive Model

Grand Challenge Technologies Ltd uses an agentic founding executive cabinet under the ultimate responsibility of Jamie Steeg, Founder & CEO (`jimsteeg`).

This document names the public operating functions. The canonical prompts, internal work queues, private decision artifacts, portfolio analysis, delegation records, and sensitive operating material are maintained in the private GCT executive repository.

## Founder & CEO

**Jamie Steeg** — Founder & CEO.

The Founder retains ultimate corporate responsibility and exercises reserved corporate authority. Agentic offices are delegated operating functions; they do not become independent legal persons or acquire authority merely by invocation.

## Operating principle

The cabinet is designed to maximize useful agentic freedom while minimizing unnecessary Founder clerical load.

Routine authorized work should proceed through analysis, planning, bounded execution, review routing, recovery, readback, and artifact maintenance without converting every step into a Founder approval gate.

Founder escalation is reserved for actual corporate/strategic boundaries such as legal or contractual commitments, material capital actions, corporate filings, major irreversible allocations, safety-critical deployments, executive appointments or compensation, material public corporate commitments, or actions outside standing delegation.

## Independence principle

Executive sponsorship is not independent review.

An office may frame, sponsor, prioritize, or commission work. It may not count its own sponsorship or implementation as independent verification, Referee disposition, MATHCERT certification, Human Steward authorization, or professional legal/accounting advice.

Where GCL governance requires independent roles, those roles remain distinct and are governed by the applicable GCL/INTELLECT/programme rules.

## Public/private split

Public by design:

- office names and mandates;
- GCT↔GCL authority boundary;
- approved public-safe operating principles;
- deliberately released mandates and status projections;
- evidence-backed public claims.

Private by default:

- executive prompts and orchestration internals;
- portfolio rankings and resource-allocation analysis;
- Founder decision queue;
- opportunity and partner pipeline;
- capital strategy;
- professional-review queue;
- internal risk and exception registers;
- pre-decisional product/venture analysis;
- confidential or sensitive corporate records.

The public model exists for legibility. It is not a duplicate executive operating system.
