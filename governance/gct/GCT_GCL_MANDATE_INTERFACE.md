# GCT → GCL Mandate Interface

## 1. Purpose

This interface separates enterprise sponsorship from epistemic or technical authority.

GCT may decide that a question, capability, product possibility, or technology deserves investment. GCL determines how accepted work is made executable and scrutinized under its applicable governance.

## 2. Direction of flow

```text
GCT enterprise question
→ executive portfolio analysis
→ GCT mandate
→ GCL intake / INTELLECT route as applicable
→ research / engineering / evidence / review
→ bounded claim or capability result
→ GCT enterprise disposition
```

## 3. GCT mandate fields

Every material mandate SHOULD identify:

- `mandate_id`;
- sponsoring GCT office(s);
- enterprise question or objective;
- strategic rationale;
- requested decision horizon;
- bounded resource envelope or explicit `UNALLOCATED` state;
- non-goals;
- required evidence class;
- acceptable failure outcome;
- confidentiality/publicity class;
- known legal, safety, security, licensing, or data constraints;
- desired return interface;
- Founder-reserved decisions anticipated, if any.

A mandate is sponsorship, not proof and not programme admission.

## 4. GCL acceptance

GCL may accept, narrow, reject, defer, or reroute a mandate according to its governance and capacity. GCT may prioritize enterprise importance but must not falsify the evidentiary state by executive direction.

Where INTELLECT applies, the work enters the constitutional lifecycle and satisfies the applicable office obligations. Where MATHCERT applies, certification remains MATHCERT-owned. Where AETHER is authoritative for production semantic state, GCT records do not substitute for AETHER facts/cuts/provenance.

## 5. Return packet

GCL returns a packet containing, as applicable:

- exact artifact/revision identities;
- reproduced capability;
- evidence summary;
- claim boundary/status;
- uncertainty and failure modes;
- cost/compute/operational observations;
- security or safety findings;
- maintenance burden;
- reusable assets;
- next discriminating test;
- certification/proof references;
- recommended technical disposition.

## 6. Enterprise disposition

GCT interprets the returned packet for enterprise purpose. Allowed dispositions include:

- `INCUBATING` — preserve option value, do not scale;
- `ACTIVE` — continue bounded investigation/development;
- `ACCELERATE` — increase authorized resources;
- `HOLD` — preserve state pending external dependency;
- `PRODUCTIZE` — open a productization workstream without changing evidence status;
- `PARTNER` — seek an external partner under separate authority;
- `SPIN_OUT_CANDIDATE` — investigate separate venture/subsidiary form;
- `NARROW` — reduce scope to the evidenced core;
- `RETIRE` — close active work and preserve what was learned.

## 7. Anti-corruption rules

GCT executive preference cannot:

- make an experiment reproducible;
- make a model safe;
- make a mathematical statement certified;
- make a prototype production-ready;
- make data rights clear;
- make a commercial forecast true.

Likewise, GCL technical success does not automatically imply a good GCT investment. Technical truth and enterprise value are separate judgments connected by this interface.

## 8. Example

```text
GCT-CSO: "Can coupled neural systems coherently accumulate task modes while phase-nulling error modes?"
GCT-CPO: identifies possible collective-computation value
GCT-CTO: identifies feasible experimental substrate
GCT-CCP: records strategic/commercial option value, without outreach

        ↓ GCT mandate

GCL: CPS / relevant programme + INTELLECT-governed work package
        ↓
controlled evidence and claim boundary
        ↓
GCT Cabinet: continue / accelerate / productize / hold / retire
```

The question may be grand. The mandate must still be finite.
