# Grand Challenge Labs

Grand Challenge Labs develops auditable, reproducible programmes. GitHub is our
operational and evidentiary substrate; it is not an independent mathematical
authority.

- The [Programme front door](https://grandchallenge.github.io/MATH-PROGRAMME/)
  and [Programme Atlas](https://grandchallenge.github.io/MATH-PROGRAMME/PROGRAMME_ATLAS/)
  provide the public map of the mathematics programme.
- [MATH-PROGRAMME](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME) is the
  mathematics-programme authority and pins adopted standards.
- [MATHFORGE](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATHFORGE) discovers and
  reconstructs candidate mathematics.
- [MATHSOLVE](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATHSOLVE) organizes disciplined
  mathematical work packages.
- [MATHCERT](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATHCERT) alone admits checked
  mathematical evidence and determines claim disposition.
- [INTELLECT](https://github.com/grandchallenge/INTELLECT) contains the compact
  Constitution and effective amendments.
- [gcl-standards](https://github.com/grandchallenge/gcl-standards) is the
  subordinate, versioned registry and publication surface for cross-programme
  standards. A candidate standard remains nonbinding until admitted by its
  superior authority and adopted through the applicable programme decision.

The mathematics authority split is specified in the
[three-pillar architecture](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE_OVERVIEW.md).
The [claim-boundary doctrine](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/blob/main/docs/CLAIM_BOUNDARY_DOCTRINE.md)
defines permitted support language, and the
[certification ladder](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/blob/main/CERTIFICATION_LADDER.md)
defines promotion gates.

Issues own next actions, pull requests integrate authoritative changes, Actions
produce evidence, Projects index work, Discussions host nonbinding
deliberation, and Releases publish admitted immutable artifacts.

Public deliberation is welcome in
[Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/grandchallenge/discussions). An accepted
proposal gains authority only after conversion to a governed issue or ADR pull
request and completion of the applicable review.
